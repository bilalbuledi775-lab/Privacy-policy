1. Overview

Tic Tac Toe ("the Game", "we", "us", or "our") is a free browser-based game that can be played locally, against a computer opponent, or online against other players.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the Game, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and what choices you have. By using the Game you agree to the practices described in this policy.

In short: We collect only what's needed to run online multiplayer and a friends system. We don't ask for your name, email, phone number, or any payment information. We don't sell your data. We don't show ads.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide

When you use the Game, you may provide:

  • Display name — a nickname you choose. This is optional and defaults to an auto-generated name. It can be anything, including a pseudonym. Please do not use your real name if you don't want it visible to other players.
  • Friend code — a 6-character code that the Game generates automatically so other players can add you as a friend.

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you use the online features, the Game automatically generates and stores:

Data Purpose
Anonymous user ID A random identifier assigned by Firebase Authentication. It is not linked to your real identity, email, or device.
Online status Whether you are currently online or offline, and the last time you were seen. Shown to your friends.
Friends list The list of players you have added as friends.
Game data Room codes, board state, scores, and match results for online games.
Technical data IP address, browser type, and general device information, collected automatically by Firebase for security and abuse prevention.

2.3 What we do not collect

We do not collect or request:

  • Your real name, email address, or phone number
  • Your physical address or precise location
  • Payment or financial information
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-site tracking data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Create and maintain your anonymous account
  • Enable online multiplayer matches
  • Let you add friends and see when they are online
  • Let friends invite you to game rooms
  • Detect and prevent abuse, cheating, or misuse of the Game
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve stability

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to running the Game.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your information under the following legal bases:

  • Contract — to provide the Game you are using
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the Game secure and prevent abuse
  • Consent — where required, such as for optional features

You may withdraw consent at any time by ceasing to use the Game.

5. How We Store and Protect Your Data

All game data is stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database, a cloud service provided by Google LLC. Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Firebase applies industry-standard security measures to protect stored data.

Access to your data is restricted by Firebase Security Rules. Other players can only see your display name, friend code, and online status. Only you can read or write your friends list.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

We keep your data only as long as necessary:

  • Anonymous account and profile — until you request deletion or the Game is discontinued
  • Game rooms — deleted automatically after 30 days of inactivity, or sooner if an administrator clears them
  • Online status — updated in real time and set to "offline" when you close the Game
  • Ban records — kept while a ban is active and removed when lifted

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any other third party, except where required by law.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

The Game does not use tracking cookies. Firebase Authentication may store a session token in your browser's local storage to keep you signed in between visits. This token is required for the Game to function and does not contain personal information.

You can clear this token at any time by clearing your browser's site data. Doing so will sign you out and generate a new anonymous account.

10. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Because the Game uses anonymous accounts and does not request any personal information, it is designed to be safe for general audiences. However, if you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will delete it promptly.

Parents: The Game includes a chat-free friends system and anonymous multiplayer. We recommend supervising younger children and reminding them not to share personal information in their display name.
